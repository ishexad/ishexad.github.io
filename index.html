About

Insight with an audience of decision-makers.

I'm a Data and Insights Analyst with over a decade of international NGO experience, including several years leading data and insights work for Oxfam International's global confederation. My work turns complex, multi-country data into clear, board-level insight — and builds the digital tools, dashboards, and training that embed a stronger data culture across teams.

Based in Preston, UK, I've led cross-functional and international work, partnered with senior leadership on strategy, and represented staff interests at board level as an elected Staff Forum Chairperson. My track record: replacing fragmented, offline processes with shared digital systems, and turning data into insight that shapes organisational decision-making.
